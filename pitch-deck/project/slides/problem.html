Warden

03

Every agent that reads the outside world is exposed. Give it an inbox, a browser or a shell, and every one of those becomes a door. A single sentence hidden in a document is enough to make it leak keys or forward invoices. Keyword filters do not help: they block innocent emails that say "ignore my previous message" and miss attacks that are reworded or encoded.
