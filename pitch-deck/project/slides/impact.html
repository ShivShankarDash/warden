Context first. Accenture's 2025 resilience study found 90 percent of organizations are not ready for AI-augmented threats, and 63 percent sit in its Exposed Zone, with neither a cohesive strategy nor the technical capability. IBM puts the average breach at 4.44 million dollars. Warden changes the economics. Run fully on-prem and the API bill is zero and no data leaves your network, which is what compliance teams need. With the optional judge on, it still costs about 79 percent less than sending every scan to a cloud model, because only about one scan in five is uncertain enough to need it. Local scans average 133 milliseconds against roughly one and a half seconds for an LLM check. One machine running one process handles about 550 thousand scans a day. Accuracy trade-off: 84.4 percent fully local, 87.6 percent with the judge on the deepset set.
