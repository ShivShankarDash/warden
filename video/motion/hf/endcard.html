Warden
A careful reader between your AI and everything it reads. Open source, local, and about five minutes to install.
Thank you for your attention.
1 · Install Bun
❯curl -fsSL https://bun.sh/install | bash
2 · Start Warden
❯bunx @shivdev/agent-warden --api-only
3 · Protect your IDE
❯bunx @shivdev/agent-warden setup --global
